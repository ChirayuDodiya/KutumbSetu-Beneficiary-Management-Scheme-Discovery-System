# Mukhyamantri Amrutum (MA) Yojana

## Overview
The Mukhyamantri Amrutum (MA) Yojana is a flagship health insurance scheme designed to provide quality medical and surgical care to families belonging to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category and lower-middle-class families. It ensures that unforeseen medical emergencies do not push vulnerable families into debt.

## Eligibility Criteria
To be eligible for the MA Yojana, families must meet the following strict conditions:
- **Income Limit**: The total annual family income must be less than or equal to ₹4,00,000.
- **Caste/Category**: This scheme is open to ALL categories (General, SC, ST, SEBC).
- **Residency**: The family must be a resident of the state.

## Benefits Provided
- Cashless medical and surgical treatment up to **₹5,00,000 per family per year**.
- Covers over 1,700 critical illnesses including cardiovascular surgeries, neurosurgeries, burns, poly-trauma, and cancer treatments.
- Includes pre-existing diseases from day one of enrollment.
- Travel allowance of ₹300 per visit for the patient for traveling to the hospital.

## Required Documents
When applying for the MA Yojana or visiting an empaneled hospital, the following documents must be verified:
1. **Income Certificate**: A valid Income Certificate issued by the Talati or Mamlatdar (mandatory to prove income < ₹4 Lakh).
2. **Aadhaar Card**: Aadhaar card of the head of the family and the patient.
3. **Ration Card**: To prove family composition and residency.

## How to Apply
Families can register at designated MA Yojana kiosks located at civil hospitals, community health centers, or specific government offices. Upon verification of the Income Certificate, a biometric MA Card with a unique QR code is issued on the spot.

## Exclusions
- Routine OPD consultations.
- Cosmetic surgeries.
- Families with an annual income strictly greater than ₹4,00,000 are completely ineligible and will be rejected.
