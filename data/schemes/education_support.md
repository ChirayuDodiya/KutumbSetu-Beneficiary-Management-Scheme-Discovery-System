# Education Support Scheme

## Process
1. Register your family on the Kutumbsetu portal.
2. Ensure your family income is below ₹1,50,000.
3. Submit the required documents under the Benefit Requests section.
4. An officer will review and approve the stipend, which will be directly transferred to your bank account.

## Required Documents
- Income Certificate
- Student Enrollment Proof (from a recognized school/college)
- Aadhaar Card of the student and head of family.

## Additional Rules
- The student must maintain at least 70% attendance.
- This scheme is valid only for students aged 5 to 25.
