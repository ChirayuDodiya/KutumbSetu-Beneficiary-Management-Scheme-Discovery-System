# Housing Assistance

## Process
1. Obtain an estimate from a certified builder for your house construction or renovation.
2. Submit the application along with land ownership proof.
3. The local talati/officer will conduct a physical verification of the land.
4. Subsidy is released in three phases based on construction progress.

## Required Documents
- Income Certificate (below ₹1,20,000)
- Caste Certificate (SC, ST, or SEBC)
- Land Ownership Document (7/12 extract or equivalent)

## Important Notes
- The house must be registered in the name of the female head of the family where possible.
