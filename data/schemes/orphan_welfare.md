# Orphan Child Welfare

## Process
1. An application must be filed by the legal guardian of the child.
2. Local authorities will verify the death certificates of both parents.
3. A monthly stipend will be activated for the child until they turn 18.

## Required Documents
- Death Certificate of both Parents
- Age Proof of the child
- Guardianship proof (if applicable)

## Scope
- Covers full school fees up to standard 12.
- Monthly allowance of ₹3,000 for nutritional support.
