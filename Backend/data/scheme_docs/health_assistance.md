# Senior Citizen Health Assistance

## Process
1. Register on the portal.
2. Apply for the scheme through the citizen dashboard.
3. Once approved by the local officer, a health card will be issued.
4. Show the health card at partnered hospitals to avail free treatments.

## Required Documents
- Age Proof (must clearly show age is 60 or above)
- Income Certificate (family income must be below ₹2,50,000)
- Any previous Medical Bills for ongoing treatments (optional but recommended)

## Benefits
- Free OPD consultations.
- Hospitalization cover up to ₹5,00,000.
